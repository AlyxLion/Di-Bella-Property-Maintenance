Hard surface cleaning like brick, Cladding, rock and concrete washing using our custom built multi use platform

Soft Wash

Some surfaces can be cleaned better using our low pressure solution based service like getting moss off your sandstone and pavers. So we provide a solution for this which acts over time to bring back the original surface of the surface and make it look as good as new

Solar panel cleaning

Did you know that most Solar Panels are coated with a special surface that prevents dirt build up and if high pressure is used it can remove this coating and damage your Expensive Solar Panels? At Di Bella Property Maintenance we provide our Di-ionised Soft Water system which uses no chemicals but highly Polished water to strip the dirt from the panels, leaving the coating undamaged saving your panels from being renewed earlier then necessary

  • Roof Treatment
